Why BubbleOS?

Built from the ground up with modern principles.

🔒

Immutable & Atomic

Root filesystem based on btrfs generations. Instant rollback if something goes wrong.

⚡

Extremely Lightweight

Desktop under 220 MB of RAM. Fast, responsive and respectful of your hardware.

📦

ULPM Package Manager

Rootless, zero-daemon, sandboxed .lpk packages. Modern alternative to Flatpak & Snap.

🪟

Wayland Native

BubbleWM compositor with custom Cyberpunk Glass theme. Smooth and beautiful.

🛡️

Secure by Design

Sandboxed apps, declarative permissions and Ed25519 signatures on every package.

🎨

Cyberpunk Aesthetic

Carefully crafted dark blue & cyan glass theme. Beautiful out of the box.

The vision

BubbleOS is an independent Linux operating system focused on simplicity, performance and modern design. It combines an atomic update model with a beautiful Wayland desktop and a next-generation package manager.

Currently in early development (v0.0.1 — Generation 01).
